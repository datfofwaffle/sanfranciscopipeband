Our mission

Teaching the next generation.

The Prince Charles Pipe Band is dedicated to teaching youth and promoting excellence in the art of Scottish bagpiping and drumming.

Three members of the City of San Francisco (Caledonian) Pipe Band started it when they saw that the Bay Area had no organization to train young pipers and drummers. Sponsored by the Caledonian Club of San Francisco, the band has been teaching and competing ever since, in the US, Canada and Scotland.
